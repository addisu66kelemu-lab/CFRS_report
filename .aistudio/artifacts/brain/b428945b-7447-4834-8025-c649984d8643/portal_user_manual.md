### 1. Introduction & Purpose

This portal serves as the unified digital hub for civil registration and vital statistics (CRVS) across all **10 Administrative Zones and Special Woredas of Central Ethiopia**:
- **Hadiya Zone** (ሆሳዕና)
- **Gurage Zone** (ወልቂጤ)
- **East Gurage Zone** (ቡታጅራ)
- **Silte Zone** (ወራቤ)
- **Kembata Zone** (ዱራሜ)
- **Halaba Zone** (ሀላባ ቁሊቶ)
- **Yem Special Zone** (ሳጃ)
- **Mareko Special Woreda/Zone** (ኮስሄ)
- **Tembaro Special Woreda/Zone** (ሙዱላ)
- **Kebena Special Woreda/Zone** (ወልቂጤ ዙሪያ)

### 2. Citizen Guide: Services, Deadlines & Verification

Citizens can access vital service information without logging in:
1. **Explore the 5 Core Vital Services**:
   - Navigate to the **አገልግሎቶች (Services)** tab.
   - Select between **ልደት (Birth)**, **ሞት (Death)**, **ጋብቻ (Marriage)**, **ፍቺ (Divorce)**, and **ጉዲፈቻ (Adoption)**.
   - Review necessary prerequisites, legal criteria, supporting documents required from health facilities or courts, and issuance steps.
2. **Statutory Grace Period & Fee Calculator**:
   - All timely registrations within statutory limits are **100% FREE (0 ETB)**.
   - Any late registration beyond the statutory deadline incurs a **500 ETB penalty fee**.
   - Use the interactive date calculator on the **መነሻ (Home)** page: enter the event's occurrence date to automatically determine whether the registration is within the free window or subject to the late penalty.
3. **Citizen Feedback & Inquiry**:
   - Go to **ያግኙን (Contact)**.
   - Fill out the feedback form with your name, phone number, relevant zone, and comments.
   - Submit to receive an official tracking ticket number (e.g., `CR-FB-8421`).

### 3. Zone Officer Guide: Monthly Reporting, Local Analytics & Printing

Zonal vital statistics coordinators use their assigned credentials to report monthly woreda aggregations to the regional center.

> **Strict Data Security & RBAC Enforcement**:
> In accordance with regional governance policy, **zone coordinators and officers outside the main regional administration cannot view aggregate cross-zonal reports or comparisons of other zones**.
> Zone officers have dedicated access strictly limited to:
> 1. **መላክ (Submit)** their own zone's monthly woreda & kebele filings.
> 2. **ማየት (View)** only their own zone's submissions, historical trends, and local statistics.
> 3. **ፕሪንት ማድረግ (Print)** official stamped and certified monthly report dossiers directly to paper or PDF.

1. **Logging In**:
   - Click **መግቢያ/Login** on the top right navigation bar.
   - Select your zone (e.g. *ምሥራቅ ጉራጌ ዞን* or *ስልጤ ዞን*) and sign in with authorized credentials.
2. **Accessing the Reporting Form**:
   - Click on the **የወሩ ሪፖርት መላኪያ** tab on the navigation bar.
3. **Statutory 3-Tier Classification for All 5 Vital Events**:
   - Input counts for each vital event categorized strictly according to statutory windows:
     - **ልደት (Birth)**:
       - **ወቅታዊ**: በተወለደ በ90 ቀናት ውስጥ የተመዘገበ (0 ETB - ነፃ)
       - **የዘገየ**: በተወለደ ከ90 ቀን እስከ 1 ዓመት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (500 ETB ቅጣት)
       - **ነባር**: በተወለደ ከ1 ዓመት በኋላ የተመዘገበ
     - **ሞት (Death)**:
       - **ወቅታዊ**: ከሞተ እስከ 50 ቀናት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (0 ETB - ነፃ)
       - **የዘገየ**: ከ50 ቀን እስከ 1 ዓመት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (500 ETB ቅጣት)
       - **ነባር**: ከ1 ዓመት በኋላ የተመዘገበ
     - **ጋብቻ (Marriage)**:
       - **ወቅታዊ**: በተጋቡ በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ የተመዘገበ (0 ETB - ነፃ)
       - **የዘገየ**: ከ31 ቀናት እስከ 1 ዓመት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (500 ETB ቅጣት)
       - **ነባር**: ከ1 ዓመት በኋላ የተመዘገበ
     - **ፍቺ (Divorce)**:
       - **ወቅታዊ**: ፍቺው በተፈጸመ በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ የተመዘገበ (0 ETB - ነፃ)
       - **የዘገየ**: ከ31 ቀናት እስከ 1 ዓመት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (500 ETB ቅጣት)
       - **ነባር**: ከ1 ዓመት በኋላ የተመዘገበ
     - **ጉዲፈቻ (Adoption)**:
       - **ወቅታዊ**: በ30 ቀናት ውስጥ የተመዘገበ (0 ETB - ነፃ)
       - **የዘገየ**: ከ31 ቀናት እስከ 1 ዓመት ባለው ጊዜ የተመዘገበ (500 ETB ቅጣት)
       - **ነባር**: ከ1 ዓመት በኋላ የተመዘገበ
4. **Official Printing (ፕሪንት ማድረግ)**:
   - Click **🖨️ የዞኑን ሪፖርት አትም (Print)** at the top of the reporting view, or click the **አትም** button on any past monthly report.
   - The modal renders a formal government dossier with the official seal, coordinator signature line, statutory breakdowns, and late penalty totals, ready to send to any local printer or save as PDF.
5. **Submitting to Regional Administrator (መላክ)**:
   - Click **ሪፖርቱን ለክልሉ አስተዳዳሪ ላክ**.
   - The submission is recorded, timestamped, and immediately archived in the zone's private filing cabinet.

### 4. Regional Admin Guide: Approval & Analytics

Regional administrators oversee and harmonize all 10 zonal branches:
1. **Reviewing Incoming Monthly Submissions**:
   - Go to **የክልሉ አስተዳደር መቆጣጠሪያ (Admin Portal)** > **የዞኖች ወርሃዊ ሪፖርት መቀበያ**.
   - Review submitted dossiers with itemized numbers, reporting coverage, and late penalties.
   - Click **አጽድቅ (Approve)** to officially validate the report.
2. **Monitoring Reporting Matrix**:
   - Toggle to **የ10ሩ ዞኖች ማረጋገጫ ማትሪክስ (Checklist Matrix)** to see which zones have submitted on time versus which are pending.
   - Click **ማሳሰቢያ ላክ (Send Reminder)** to send automated alerts to pending zones.
3. **Seasonal Trend Analysis via Recharts**:
   - Navigate to the **ሪፖርቶች (Reports)** tab or the Admin **የምዝገባ ስታቲስቲክስና ግራፎች (Charts)** tab.
   - Inspect the monthly line chart showing registration trends across the 4 Ethiopian seasons:
     - **ፀደይ (Tsedey / Post-Harvest)**: High birth registrations, favorable mobility.
     - **በጋ (Bega / Dry Season)**: Peak marriage season.
     - **በልግ (Belg / Minor Rains)**: Steady trends across agricultural zones.
     - **ክረምት (Kiremt / Heavy Rains)**: Muddy roads, lower on-time clinic access, higher late registrations.
   - Switch between **ጠቅላላ ልደትና ሞት**, **መደበኛና ዘግይቶ ዝርዝር**, and **የተጣራ እድገት (Net Growth)** modes.

### 5. Super Admin Guide: Document Uploads & CMS Content Management

Super Admins hold full system authority over documentation and public communications:
1. **Uploading Documents from Local Storage**:
   - Super Admins can upload official documents directly from their computer:
     - **From Resources View**: Click the green **ሰነድ ከኮምፒውተር ይጫኑ (Upload Document)** button at the top of the Resources page.
     - **From Admin Portal**: Go to the **📁 የሰነዶች መጫኛ (Document Upload)** tab and click **ሰነድ ከኮምፒውተር ጫን**.
     - **From CMS Module Editor**: Open the Resources module and click **ከኮምፒውተር ሰነድ ጫን**.
   - **Upload Modal Steps**:
     1. Click the file selector or drag and drop a PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX), or image file.
     2. The system auto-detects file name, file size, format, and suggests titles.
     3. Select the document category (*ህጋዊ አዋጅ, የክልሉ መመሪያ, የጤና ዘርፍ መመሪያ, የህዝብ ቅጽ, ወርሃዊ ሪፖርት, የዳሰሳ ጥናት*).
     4. Set the publication date in Ethiopian Calendar and enter a concise description.
     5. Click **ወደ ድረ-ገጹ ጫን (Upload to Website)**.
   - The file is stored in browser **IndexedDB**, guaranteeing reliable offline downloads for citizens without quota limits.
2. **Editing Website Content (CMS Editor)**:
   - Click **ይዘት አርትዕ (CMS)** on the navigation bar.
   - Switch between tabs: *መነሻ (Home), ስለ እኛ (About), አገልግሎቶች (Services), መመሪያዎች (Resources), ያግኙን (Contact)*.
   - Modify hero banners, announcements, hotline numbers, directives, and contact details.
   - Click **ለውጦቹን መዝግብና አስቀምጥ (Save Changes)** to apply updates across the site in real time.
   - Super Admins can revert to factory defaults at any time using **ወደ ነባሪ መልስ (Reset All Modules)**.

### 6. Report Generation: Official PDF, PowerPoint & CSV Exports

From the **ሪፖርቶች (Reports)** view:
1. **Official Regional PDF Report (jsPDF)**:
   - Includes regional heraldic seal, Ethiopian fiscal period metadata, summary cards, and zone-by-zone statistical tables ready for official printing or distribution.
2. **Executive Presentation (PPTX)**:
   - Generates multi-slide presentation decks with key performance indicators, on-time registration percentages, and regional rankings suitable for council presentations.
3. **Data Raw Dump (UTF-8 CSV)**:
   - Exports raw, machine-readable comma-separated values compatible with Excel, SPSS, and Stata for in-depth epidemiological and demographic research.

### 7. Security Administration & Credential Rotation Guidelines

For production deployment and ongoing operation:
1. **Masked Password Security**:
   - In the **ዞኖችና ይለፍ ቃላት (Zone Credentials)** tab of the Admin Portal, all passwords are automatically masked with dots (`••••••••••••`).
   - Click the eye icon toggle next to any password to temporarily inspect the credential when communicating with zonal bureau officers.
2. **Rotating Zone & Admin Passwords**:
   - Click **ቀይር (Change Password)** next to any zonal or administrative account.
   - Enter a secure new password (minimum 6 characters).
   - The system immediately commits the change, logs a timestamped audit entry (`SECURITY_PASSWORD_UPDATED`), and ensures future logins require the updated secret.
3. **Brute Force Protection**:
   - The login dialog automatically enforces a velocity lockout: 5 consecutive invalid authentication attempts will lock access for 30 seconds to mitigate automated credential stuffing.
4. **Clean Production State**:
   - All sample/mock submissions and artificial citizen entries have been completely removed.
   - Any new records entered by the 10 zones or citizens will constitute authentic operational civil registry data.
